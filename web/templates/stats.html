Features Used

  • World Ranking Differential - HLTV official ranking gap
  • Win Rate (3 months) - Recent overall win rate differential
  • Recent Form - Performance in last 10 matches
  • Head-to-Head - Historical matchup advantage
  • Map Win Rate - Map-specific performance differential

  • LAN Factor - LAN vs online context
  • Experience - Matches played differential
  • Win Streak - Current streak momentum

Model

XGBoost Gradient Boosted Trees with hyperparameter tuning. Fallback: weighted heuristic using sigmoid-transformed feature combination.
